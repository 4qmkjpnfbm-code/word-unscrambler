About

About this word unscrambler

This site does one job well: turn a jumble of letters into every valid English word you can make. No account, no install, no newsletter, and no server-side lookup of your rack.

How solving works

When you open the tool, the browser loads the ENABLE word list once. After that, every search runs on your device. That means your letters never leave the page as a query string to our backend, and the tool still works on a train with a shaky signal after the first load.

Modes cover the usual needs: All words (subsets for Scrabble-style racks), Anagrams (use every letter — newspaper jumbles), Wordle (greens, yellows, greys), and Pattern (crossword masks with ? for blanks). Use up to two ? wildcards for blank tiles.

Dictionary and scores

The dictionary is ENABLE — public-domain English with about 168,000 entries. QI, ZA, JO, XI and the usual short plays are included. Tournament lists such as NASPA TWL and Collins CSW are copyrighted, so we do not copy them. ENABLE is close enough for casual Scrabble and Words With Friends; if a club uses a different book, check a disputed word before you challenge.

Scoring uses standard English Scrabble tile values by default, with an optional Words With Friends table. Board premiums (double word, triple letter) are not applied because we cannot see your board. See also Scrabble vs Words With Friends and how blank tiles work.
