Find Words With Friends plays without leaving the table

Words With Friends rewards the same craft as kitchen-table Scrabble: stretch a rack, hook a lane, and know when a short dump beats a glamorous dud. This page opens the shared ENABLE solver with Words With Friends tile values in mind so the ranking feels closer to Zynga’s board than to classic Scrabble. Letters stay in your browser. There is no account wall and no upload of your chat thread.

WWF’s board premiums and tile bag differ from Hasbro Scrabble. We do not paint your board — only you can see the lane — but we can list every ENABLE word your rack can make and sort by the WWF point table. That is enough to stop guessing whether QI is worth the swap.

Worked ENABLE rack examples

Rack: AEINRST (the classic stem). In subset mode the helper surfaces TRAIN, TRAINS, RETAINS, STAINER, RETINAS and many shorter dumps. Full anagrams of all seven include RETAINS, STAINER, STEARIN and similar ENABLE entries — see also the crawlable list at /unscramble/aeinrst. On WWF scoring, high-value consonants still dominate the sort when J, Q, X or Z appear.

Rack: Q?IIZU with a blank. Treat ? as the blank. ENABLE’s QI and QUIZ-family plays matter; QI alone is a legal two-letter dump on this dictionary. If the board offers a double-letter on Z, QUIZ-shaped words leap the ranking even when the raw rack looked ugly.

Rack: LISTEN. Subsets include SILENT’s shorter cousins LIST, LINT, TINS, and the full anagrams SILENT, ENLIST, INLETS, TINSEL, ELINTS. For a pure anagram challenge open jumble mode; for board dumps stay on All words here or on the homepage unscrambler.

Scrabble vs Words With Friends on this site

Tile values diverge (WWF weighs some consonants differently). Our Scrabble vs WWF guide lists the practical gaps. The Scrabble word finder keeps classic values; this page is the WWF-flavoured door. Neither page copies a proprietary lexicon — both use ENABLE — so a club challenge book may still disagree on an obscure word.

Board craft the solver cannot see

Premium squares, hooks and blockers are yours to judge. A 20-point word that opens a triple-word lane for your opponent can be a bad trade. Use the list to invent candidates, then look at the board. Bingo stems such as those on bingo stems help you notice seven-letter shapes faster. Short power plays live on J, Q, X and Z words and Q without U.

Worked endgame dump

Late-game racks often look ugly: two vowels, a duplicate consonant, and a lonely high tile. Take EEAILQ. ENABLE still offers QI if a lane exists, plus ALEE, AIL, EEL and LEA dumps that clear vowels before the bag locks you. Sorting by WWF values surfaces QI near the top when Q is playable; if the board blocks Q, take the vowel clear and live to chase a better lane next turn.

Another rack: TIONERS fragments. Players hunting bingo shapes will recognise how TION and ERS invite longer builds. Cross-check candidates on the bingo stems page, then return here to score the actual rack you hold.

FAQ

Is this the official WWF dictionary?

No. It uses ENABLE. Casual play matches closely; tournament or in-app challenges may differ.

Do you apply board multipliers?

No. We cannot see your board. Scores are tile sums with the WWF weight table.

FAQ

How is Words With Friends scoring different from Scrabble?

Tile values differ. In WWF, J is 10 (8 in Scrabble), L is 2 (1), and several other letters change. This page uses the WWF table.

Does the score include the bingo bonus?

No. Points shown are tile values only. Add 35 yourself if you used all seven tiles on a Words With Friends board.

Is this the official WWF word list?

No. ENABLE is public-domain English. Zynga’s list is separate. If a club forbids a word, trust the game, not this page.
