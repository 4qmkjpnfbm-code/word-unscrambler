FAQ

What words can you make with CREDIT?

ENABLE lists 54. Three six-letter anagrams: credit, direct and triced. Useful fives: cider, cited, tired, tried, edict.

Is DIRECT an anagram of CREDIT?

Yes. Same six letters. Newspaper clues about a path or instruction usually want DIRECT; clues about money want CREDIT.

What does TRICED mean?

Hauled up and secured with a trice, a nautical term. Valid in ENABLE. Jumble clues about ropes or ships sometimes want TRICED.
