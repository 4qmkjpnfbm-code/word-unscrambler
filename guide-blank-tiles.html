Blank tiles and the ? wildcard

A blank tile can stand for any letter. On this site type ? in the letters box — each question mark is one blank. You can use up to two.

How to search with blanks

  1. Type your known letters, then add ? for each blank (max two).
  2. Example: A?PLE finds apple, ample and other five-letter fits.
  3. Example: QI? finds words that start with QI plus any third letter that forms a valid word.

Blanks score zero points in Scrabble and Words With Friends. This tool lists every ENABLE word your rack can form; it does not place tiles on a board, so board premiums are not applied.

Why only two blanks?

Three or more wildcards explode the search space. Two is enough for a normal rack and keeps results instant in the browser.
