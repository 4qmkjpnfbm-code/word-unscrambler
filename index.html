Make words from any jumble of letters

This word unscrambler checks your letters against the ENABLE English word list in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to a server. A match is any word whose letters can be formed from your rack, including shorter words — the same way a Scrabble rack works.

Need the exact rearrangement only? Switch to Anagrams only. Solving a daily puzzle? Open the Wordle helper or the jumble solver. Playing a board game? Use the Scrabble word finder for tile scores and bingo highlights. Stuck on a crossword? Try the crossword solver with ? for unknown squares.

Blank tiles are ?. Filters keep words that start with, end with, or contain a pattern — useful for hooks, stems and grey Wordle letters (Exclude).

Worked example: LISTEN

Type LISTEN and ENABLE returns 92 words. The six anagrams that use every letter are silent, listen, enlist, tinsel, inlets and elints. Shorter plays include isle, tile, sent and the two-letter words in, is, it, el.

Other crawlable examples: AEINRST, SCRABBLE, TRAIN, EARTH, ADOBE, RACE, RETINA. Full index: unscramble examples.

FAQ

What is a word unscrambler?

A word unscrambler takes a set of letters and lists every valid English word you can form from them, including shorter words. Use it for Scrabble, Words With Friends, Wordle, jumbles and crossword patterns. This site checks the ENABLE list (~168,000 words) in your browser and scores plays with Scrabble or Words With Friends points.

Is this free?

Yes. No account, no install. The solver stays free. Ads may be added later; they will never block results.

Can I use it for Wordle?

Yes. Switch to Wordle mode, type greens in the five boxes, put yellows in the letters field, and grey letters in Exclude. You can also browse all 5-letter words.

How do blank tiles work?

Type ? for each blank (max two), or tap Add blank ?. The solver tries every letter in that slot — the same as a Scrabble blank.

How do I find the highest scoring word?

Results sort by Scrabble points by default. Bingos (using every tile, 7+ letters) are marked. Switch Score to WWF if you play Words With Friends.

Anagram vs unscrambler?

Unscrambler lists every word you can make, including shorter ones. Anagrams only keep words that use every letter — LISTEN → SILENT, ENLIST, TINSEL.

Is my data collected?

Queries stay on your device. See the privacy policy for hosting logs and future ads.
