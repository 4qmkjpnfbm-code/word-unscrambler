Unscramble EAGLE

People type “unscramble eagle” when a crossword, kids’ jumble or classroom anagram uses the bird. ENABLE keeps two five-letter anagrams — eagle and aglee (on the lee side, a sailing word) — and 25 words in total from the letters E A G L E.

The double E is the constraint. You cannot play a word that needs a third E, and GALE uses only one of them. GLEE and AGEE keep both. Two-letter stock is ae, ag, al, ea, ee, el, la — useful when the board is tight and you only need a hook.

How to play EAGLE

In a newspaper jumble the answer is almost always EAGLE (the bird) rather than AGLEE. In Scrabble both score 6 as a dump before board premiums. GALE on a double-word square often beats the full anagram. If you drew an R you can stretch toward LARGE or GLARE in the live solver; this page only uses the five letters written in the heading.

Why 25 words, not “every English word”

We use ENABLE, a public-domain list of about 168,000 words. Collins and NASPA TWL differ on a handful of slang and names. AGLEE is valid here. If a club forbids a word, trust the tournament book, not this page.

FAQ

What words can you make with EAGLE?

ENABLE lists 25 words. The two full anagrams are eagle and aglee. Useful shorter takes: gale, glee, agee, alee, egal, and the two-letter dumps ag, el, la.

Is AGLEE a real word?

Yes in ENABLE. Aglee means on the lee side — away from the wind. Jumble books almost always want EAGLE instead. Open AGLEE in the solver.

How do I add a blank or a sixth letter?

This page is the five tiles only. Type EAGLE? in the live unscrambler to treat a blank as a sixth letter.
