Scrabble vs Words With Friends

Same idea — make words from a rack — but scores, boards and official word lists differ. This site scores with either table and checks the public ENABLE list.

Letter scores

Scrabble (English): 1 — A E I O U L N R S T · 2 — D G · 3 — B C M P · 4 — F H V W Y · 5 — K · 8 — J X · 10 — Q Z. Blank is 0.

Words With Friends: several letters differ (for example B, C, M, P, H, V, Y, K and J/X/Q/Z values). Use the WWF toggle on the tool when you need those points.

Neither mode adds double-word or triple-letter premiums — we cannot see your board.

Dictionaries

  • ENABLE — public-domain English; what this unscrambler uses (~168,000 words). QI, ZA, JO, XI and similar short plays are included.
  • TWL / OTCWL — North American tournament Scrabble (copyrighted).
  • CSW / SOWPODS — Collins list used in the UK and international play (copyrighted).

We do not ship TWL or CSW. If a club uses a different book, verify a disputed word before you challenge.
