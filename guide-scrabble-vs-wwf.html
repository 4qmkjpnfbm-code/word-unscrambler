Scrabble vs Words With Friends

Same rack, different points. This site can score either table. It always checks ENABLE, not the official word list of either game.

English Scrabble face values

This is the default. 1 — A E I L N O R S T U. 2 — D G. 3 — B C M P. 4 — F H V W Y. 5 — K. 8 — J X. 10 — Q Z. A blank is 0.

Words With Friends face values

Switch Score to WWF under More options. 1 — A E I O R S T. 2 — D L N U. 3 — G H Y. 4 — B C F M P W. 5 — K V. 8 — X. 10 — J Q Z. A blank is 0.

The letters that change a close decision are the ones that moved. L and N are 1 in Scrabble and 2 in Words With Friends, so a dump such as IN or ON is worth more in the app. J is 8 in Scrabble and 10 in Words With Friends. Y is 4 in Scrabble and 3 in Words With Friends. A word that ranks first on the Scrabble table can rank second once you flip the toggle. Flip it before you trust the order.

Neither table adds a double-word square, a triple-letter square, or the 50-point bingo for using all seven tiles. Those depend on the board. The number here is the sum of the tiles, so you can rank the rack. You still have to look at the square.

The lists are not the same either

Scores and dictionaries are separate problems. ENABLE is the public-domain list this site uses, about 168,000 words, including QI, ZA, JO and XI. North American tournament Scrabble uses NASPA TWL. UK and international tournament play uses Collins CSW. Both of those books are copyrighted, so we do not copy them.

Casual games are usually close enough. A rated game is not. If the word is the one that wins the argument, check it in the book your club actually uses before you challenge. The two-letter list is the ENABLE set: 124 words. QI and ZA are the 11-point Scrabble plays, with no blank.
