How the square works

Rivals rank for “letter boxed solver” and “letter boxed hints” with daily NYT answer pages. We do not publish today’s NYT answers. This is a solver: paste any 12 unique letters, 3 per side.

Rules used here: 12 unique letters; words 3 letters or longer; consecutive letters must sit on different sides; letters may be reused in later words. A solution is a chain where the last letter of one word is the first letter of the next, covering all 12 letters. The suggested chain is greedy, not always the shortest.

FAQ

Is this the New York Times Letter Boxed?

No. Not affiliated. Paste any square to practise. ENABLE is public-domain; NYT uses its own word list, so some ENABLE words will not count officially.

How do I enter the sides?

Type 12 letters clockwise from the top side (3+3+3+3), or fill the Top / Right / Bottom / Left boxes. Each side is three letters, all 12 unique.

What is a chain?

A sequence of legal words where each word starts with the last letter of the one before it, and together they use every letter on the square at least once. This page suggests one greedy chain; better chains may exist.
