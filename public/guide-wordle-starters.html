Guide

Wordle starting words

A good opener tests common vowels and consonants. Popular choices include SLATE, CRANE, TRACE, SALET and ADIEU. There is no single best word — pick one you will stick with.

How to use this Wordle helper

  1. Open Wordle helper or switch the main tool to Wordle mode.
  2. Enter green letters in the right positions.
  3. Put yellow letters in the letters box (known in the word, wrong place).
  4. Exclude grey letters so they never appear in results.

Results are five-letter ENABLE words that match your clues. The official Wordle list is not identical to ENABLE, so treat this as a helper, not a guarantee of every NYT answer.

After the first guess

Update greens and greys after each row. Narrowing filters is faster than guessing from memory. For pure anagrams of leftover letters, switch to Anagrams mode on the main unscrambler.
