Unscramble LISTEN

LISTEN is the rack most people type first. In ENABLE it makes six full anagrams — silent, listen, enlist, tinsel, inlets and elints — plus 86 shorter words. That mix is why teachers, jumble solvers and Scrabble coaches all use it as a demo: you see anagrams and leftover tiles on one screen.

The six-letter set is L I S T E N. Every word below uses only those letters, never more of any letter than you have. TILE, ISLE, SENT and LINE are typical four-letter takes. Two-letter plays (in, is, it, el, en, et, li, ne, si, te) matter in endgames when the board is tight.

How to play LISTEN

In a newspaper jumble, the answer is usually one of the six anagrams. In Scrabble you more often play a four- or five-letter word to hit a double-word square. SILENT and ENLIST both score 6 as a dump (no premiums). Adding a blank or an extra tile in the live solver is how you hunt a bingo from this stem.

Why this list is 92 words, not “every English word”

We use ENABLE, a public-domain list of about 168,000 words. Collins and NASPA TWL differ on a handful of slang and names. QI and ZA are in ENABLE; some proper nouns are not. If a club forbids a word, trust the tournament book, not this page.

FAQ

How many words can you make with LISTEN?

ENABLE lists 92. Six full anagrams: silent, listen, enlist, tinsel, inlets, elints. The rest are shorter leftovers such as tile, isle, sent and line.

What is the best LISTEN anagram for a jumble?

Newspaper jumbles almost always want SILENT or LISTEN. ENLIST and TINSEL appear when the clue is “join up” or “Christmas decoration”. This page lists all six so you can match the clue yourself.
