Q without U words

English almost always follows Q with U. These 23 ENABLE words do not. They let you play the 10-point Q when the U-tile is missing. Memorise QI and QAT first.

Worked example: a rack of Q I A T N with no U. Type QIATN in the unscrambler. You should see QI (11), QAT (12) and QATS if an S is on the board. Or type Q? to treat a blank as the second letter.

The four to learn this week

QI — 11 points
Two letters. Variant of chi, the life force. The most-played Q dump in Scrabble.
QAT — 12 points
Three letters. An evergreen shrub (also spelled khat). Easy to hook an S for QATS.
QOPH — 18 points
Four letters. The 19th letter of the Hebrew alphabet. Keep QOPHS in mind for the S-hook.
TRANQ — 14 points
Five letters. Short for tranquilliser. Useful when the rack is T R A N plus Q.

FAQ

Can you play Q without U in Scrabble?

Yes. ENABLE lists 23 words that contain Q but not U. QI is the two-letter dump. QAT, QOPH and TRANQ cover three to five letters. Official TWL and Collins lists are close; check your club list if you play tournament.

What does QI mean?

QI is a spelling of chi — the life force in Chinese philosophy. It is valid in ENABLE and scores 11 points before board premiums. Open QI in the unscrambler.

How do I search my rack for these?

Put Q on the rack and leave U off. Or type Q? if you have a blank. Filter length 2 to hide longer dumps while you learn QI.
