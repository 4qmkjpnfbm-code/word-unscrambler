FAQ

How many 2-letter words are there in Scrabble?

ENABLE lists 124. Official North American TWL and UK/world CSW lists are close, but not identical. Learn QI and ZA first — they dump the Q and Z.

Which two-letter words score the most?

QI and ZA are 11 points before board multipliers. JO is 9, XI and XU are 9. Vowels like AE, AI, OE are cheap but win endgames.
