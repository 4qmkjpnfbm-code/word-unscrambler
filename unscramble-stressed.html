FAQ

What is the anagram of STRESSED?

DESSERTS. ENABLE lists those two eight-letter words and 88 shorter leftovers (90 in total), including desert, dessert, dresses and tresses.

Why is STRESSED a famous anagram?

STRESSED backwards is DESSERTS. Teachers and jumble columns use it as the stock example of a surprising full anagram.

Are there other eight-letter anagrams?

Not in ENABLE. DESTRESS is often mentioned online but is not in this dictionary, so it is not on this page.
