How many 5 letter words start with S?

1,056 ENABLE five-letter words start with S. Wordle’s answer list is smaller, but this helper searches the full ENABLE five-letter set.

How do I filter 5 letter words starting with a letter plus yellows?

Open the Wordle helper. Type greens in the five boxes, yellows in Letters, greys in Grey letters. Or type a starting letter here and tap 5.

Is this the official Wordle dictionary?

No. ENABLE has 8,636 five-letter words; Wordle uses a curated answer list plus allowed guesses. ENABLE is a strong superset for “is this a valid 5-letter guess?”.

Which first letter has the fewest 5-letter words?

X has 13, Q has 53, Z has 51. S is the largest at 1,056.
