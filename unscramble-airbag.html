Unscramble AIRBAG

AIRBAG is a six-letter rack people type after a car-themed jumble or a crossword anagram. ENABLE has no six-letter anagram of A I R B A G — airbag itself is not in ENABLE as one play. What you do get is 35 shorter words, led by the five-letter AGRIA and the four-letter set BRAG, GRAB, GARB, BRIG, ARIA.

The double A is the constraint. ABA, AGA, BAA and RAGA spend both. BRAG and GRAB spend only one. Two-letter stock is aa, ab, ag, ai, ar, ba, bi, gi — GI and AA are the ones newer players miss.

How to play AIRBAG

A jumble that wants “air bag” as two words will not be satisfied by any single ENABLE word on this page. In Scrabble you are hunting BRAG / GRAB / GARB (7 points as dumps) or AGRIA if the board accepts the five. Hooking an S onto BRAG or GRAB is usually better than forcing a six that is not in the list.

Why AIRBAG itself is missing

FAQ

Can you unscramble AIRBAG into one six-letter word?

Not in ENABLE. There is no six-letter anagram. The longest play is the five-letter AGRIA. The useful fours are brag, grab, garb, brig, aria, agar, abri, raga, ragi, raia.

What does AGRIA mean?

Agria is a type of potato and, in older medical use, a pustular eruption. It is valid in ENABLE. Confirm against Collins or TWL if you play tournament.
