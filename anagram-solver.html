What an anagram solver does

An anagram is a rearrangement that uses every letter exactly once. LISTEN becomes SILENT, ENLIST, INLETS and TINSEL. This page defaults to that mode so newspaper jumbles and riddles stay uncluttered.

If you are playing Scrabble you usually want shorter words as well. Use the word unscrambler or tap All words above. For a seven-letter bingo hunt, see the Scrabble word finder.
