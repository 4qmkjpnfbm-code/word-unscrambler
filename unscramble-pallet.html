Unscramble PALLET

PALLET is a warehouse-and-art jumble: a wooden shipping platform, or an artist’s board. ENABLE keeps the word itself as the only six-letter anagram and adds 62 words from P A L L E T. The five-letter cluster is the useful one — plate, petal, pleat, lapel, leapt, palet, lepta, tepal.

The double L is the constraint. PALL, TALL, TELL and LAPEL spend both. PLATE and PETAL spend one. Two-letter stock is ae, al, at, ea, el, et, la, pa, pe, ta, te — PA and PE are the ones casual players forget.

How to play PALLET

A newspaper jumble that wants the shipping platform will accept PALLET. One that wants a flower will want PETAL; one that wants tableware will want PLATE. Scrabble does not care about the clue. PLEAT and LAPEL both score 7 as dumps; PALLET scores 8. Premiums on the board decide, not the order on this page.

Close cousins

Drop an L and you are in PLATE / PETAL / PLEAT territory. Add an S in the live solver for PALLETS, PLATES, PETALS. Related nature and object jumbles: PLANET, EARTH, ORANGE.

FAQ

What words can you make with PALLET?

ENABLE lists 62. The full anagram is pallet. Best fives: plate, petal, pleat, lapel, leapt, palet, lepta, tepal. Fours include pale, peal, pelt, tall, tell, late, tape.

Is PALET a real word?

Yes in ENABLE. Palet is a variant of pallet in the heraldry / plate sense. Tournament lists may differ — check Collins or TWL if you play club games.

Plate, petal and pleat — which should I play?

Same letters, same 7-point dump. Play the one that hits a premium or sets a hook. Open PLATE, PETAL or PLEAT in the solver to add tiles.
